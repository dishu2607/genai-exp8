Economics for Engineers is a Humanities and Social Sciences cour se being offered to 3 rd and 4 th
semester B. Tech. students as a sequel of three courses of 2 credits each.
Economics for Engineers is designed to provide engineering students with basic knowledge and
understanding of economic principles, concepts, and tools. The course aims to enhance their decision -
making skills by integrating economic analysis into engineering problem-solving. This course highlights
the relevance of economics in various engineering contexts and equips students with the necessary skills
to evaluate economic feasibility, cost -benefit analysis, and project evaluation. Throughout the course,
students will engage in case studies, discussions, and practical exercises to apply economic concepts to
engineering projects.
Pre-requisite Course Codes, if any.
Course Objective:
Course Outcomes (CO): At the end of the course students will be able to
CO1 Understand the fundamental economic concepts applicable to engineering.
CO2 Analyze and evaluate the economic feasibility of engineering projects.
CO3 Apply economic tools to make informed engineering decisions.
CO4 Identify economic factors affecting engineering industries and sectors.

The course "Law for Engineers" falls under the Humanities and Social Sciences category and is available
to 3rd and 4th semester B.Tech. students. It builds upon two previous courses, each worth 2 credits.
Through this curriculum, students gain essential insights into Indian legal principles crucial for their
future roles as engineers. Topics covered include an overview of the Indian Legal System, exploration
of jurisprudential theories, corporate and commercial laws, information technology law, Intellectual
Property Rights, and other pertinent Indian laws pertinent to engineering practices.
Pre-requisite Course Codes, if any. Nil

CO1 Recognize and investigate fundamental aspects and structures of the Indian constitution.
CO2 Distinguish and establish connections between the operations of legal systems.
